# Rank image assets

The browser-served copies are in `frontend/public/assets/membership/`.

## Pricing badges
Use only on membership pricing, benefit overview, and related marketing pages. Display the full banner with `object-fit: contain` and preserve its aspect ratio.

- `pricing/bronze.png` — Đồng
- `pricing/silver.png` — Bạc
- `pricing/gold.png` — Vàng
- `pricing/diamond.png` — Kim cương

## Avatar frames
Transparent PNG frames for member avatars. Keep the user photo centered inside the transparent opening. Do not use pricing banners as avatar frames.

- `avatar-frames/bronze.png`
- `avatar-frames/silver.png`
- `avatar-frames/gold.png`
- `avatar-frames/diamond.png`
